FAQ

Common questions.

Does FitTracker keep working when the gym has no signal?

Yes. Active workouts keep logging offline and sync automatically once your connection returns.

What does the Android app add compared to the web app?

The web app can be installed as a PWA on your device. The Android app adds a step counter, home-screen widgets, and planner reminders.

Can AI features edit my meals or routines automatically?

No. Meal photo scans and Coach proposals always appear as suggested cards so you can review and choose Accept or Dismiss before anything is saved.

Can I export my data and use my own AI key?

Yes. You can export all of your data, your progress photos stay private, and you can connect your own AI key in Settings.
